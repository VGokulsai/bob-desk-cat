# Bob's body (the case)

Decided 4 Nov 2026: **option 1**. The round 1.28" screen is his face, the real servo ears sit on top,
and the body is a case around the board. See `face/screen-options.png` for the three options compared.

## How it gets made

1. Design it in **Tinkercad** (free, in the browser): drag shapes together and export an STL.
2. Get an instant online quote and have it delivered (no printer needed nearby):
   - Robu.in 3D printing service (PLA, same shop as the parts cart)
   - Hyderabad: 3Ding, Think3D, Makenica (says overnight delivery in Hyderabad)
   - Rough guess ₹3-5 per gram of PLA, so maybe ₹500-1,000. Quote 2-3 places with the same file.
3. Ask in #fallen-help whether the printed case can go in bom.csv on the grant (not confirmed).
4. Backups: Hack Club Solvable (free print + ship, but India shipping and open status not confirmed),
   or handmade from EVA craft foam or air-dry clay.

**When:** after the PCB is ordered. Design it while the board ships, then measure the real parts.

## How to make it look good

Design him like a **vinyl toy**, not a copy of the drawing (the sketchy lines and skinny legs won't print,
and a thin body can't hold the 73 x 63 mm board).

- **Big round head, small chubby body.** The screen fills most of the face. Big-head proportions read as
  cute and leave room for the board.
- **Smooth simple shapes.** Rounded egg body, bump paws, a curled tail flat against his side.
  No thin parts that can snap.
- **White PLA + a little paint.** Pink paw pads and inner ears to match the drawing. Sand lightly first
  to hide the print lines.
- **The real ears do the talking.** The tall pink servo ears are the drawing's signature, so the body
  can stay simple.

**Avoid:** sharp boxy edges, a gap around the screen, bare servos showing. Use a rounded rim around the
screen and sleeves over the servos.
